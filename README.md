### An Ongoing Research through Design Investigation

**Chess with Ferdowsi** is an ongoing Research through Design investigation into how physical and virtual artefacts shape embodied interaction with cultural narratives.


## Live Project Page

https://aylarfakheriyan.github.io/Chess-with-Ferdowsi-/

---

The project uses the *Shahnameh* as design material rather than subject matter, developing ceramic performance artefacts and an immersive XR study to investigate how materiality, embodiment, performance, and interpretation change across physical and virtual configurations.

---

## Research Question

> **How can an artefact reshape embodied interpretation, behaviour, and experience?**

The project investigates how changing the configuration of an artefact may alter the conditions within which embodied interaction emerges.

Rather than treating performance as a predetermined outcome, the research observes how performers respond to the conditions introduced by the artefact.

---

## Research Context

The project began with a close reading of the *Shahnameh* and an interest in its representations of mythical beings, particularly the Divs.

Rather than reproducing the creatures described in the text, the project explored what the narrative leaves open for interpretation.

The *Shahnameh* speaks extensively about human experiences such as love, hatred, greed, jealousy, conflict, and war. This raised a design question:

> **What if the Divs could also embody such human conditions?**

The resulting concepts were developed as performance artefacts rather than direct visual reconstructions of the creatures in the text.

The *Shahnameh* therefore became **design material rather than subject matter**.

---

## From Cultural Narrative to Artefact

The project translates selected conceptual qualities into physical artefacts that can be worn and experienced by performers.

The intention was not to design a fixed performance or prescribe how the performers should move.

Instead, the artefacts were designed to introduce physical conditions that performers would have to negotiate through their bodies.

This created a research system in which:

**Shahnameh → Artefact → Performer → Environment → Performance**

The *Shahnameh* provides the cultural and conceptual context.

The artefact acts as a mediator between the cultural material and the performer.

The performer provides the embodied agency through which the artefact is experienced.

The environment forms part of the conditions surrounding the interaction.

Performance becomes the observable phenomenon through which changes in embodied interaction can be examined.

---

## Physical Artefact Study

The first study developed a series of ceramic performance masks.

Ceramic was selected because its material qualities could become part of the performers' experience.

The masks introduced qualities including:

* Weight
* Texture
* Physical resistance
* Fragility
* Material presence

The artefacts were designed to be worn rather than simply viewed.

Performers were invited to perform with the masks, allowing the physical qualities of the artefacts to become part of the performance conditions.

The study was explored across different environments, including the historic Qadamgah site, Tabriz Bazaar, and studio settings.

Performance conditions without the masks were also used as a point of comparison.

---

## Embodied Performance

Wearing the masks changed the performers' experience of acting.

Compared with rehearsals without the masks, performers became more aware of their movement, balance, surrounding space, and the physical condition of the artefacts.

The masks did not prescribe a specific movement.

Instead, they introduced new conditions that performers continuously negotiated during the act.

> **The artefact does not design the performance. It changes the conditions under which performance emerges.**

The resulting performance was therefore treated as observed data rather than as something completely predetermined by the designer.

## The Environment as a Variable

The physical study was not limited to a single performance setting.

Performances were explored across different environments, including:

**Qadamgah Historical Site**
**Tabriz Bazaar**
**Studio**

The environment was treated as part of the wider system surrounding the artefact and performer.

This allowed the physical study to consider how embodied interaction may emerge not only from the artefact itself, but also from the conditions in which it is encountered.

---

## From Physical to Virtual

The ceramic study raised a further question:

> **What happens when the material disappears?**

If weight, texture, fragility, and physical resistance contribute to the conditions of performance, what happens when those material qualities are removed or transformed?

To explore this question, the artefacts are being recreated digitally and introduced into an immersive XR environment.

The intention is not to change the cultural narrative or character, but to change the configuration of the artefact itself.

The physical configuration becomes virtual.

The research question remains comparable.

---

## XR Artefact Study — Ongoing

The virtual configuration is currently under development.

The XR study is designed to investigate how the same conceptual artefacts operate when physical materiality is replaced by virtual embodiment.

The development uses:

* Blender
* Unity
* OpenXR
* XR Interaction Toolkit

The final comparative evaluation will be added after the XR performance study is completed.

---

## Comparative Framework

The project explores two configurations of the artefact:

| Physical Configuration  | Virtual Configuration     |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Ceramic                 | XR                        |
| Physical weight         | Virtual presence          |
| Physical texture        | Perceptual representation |
| Fragility               | Digital condition         |
| Physical resistance     | Virtual interaction       |
| Embodied interaction    | Embodied interaction      |
| Cultural interpretation | Cultural interpretation   |

The purpose of the comparison is not to determine which medium is better.

Instead, the study asks how changing the conditions of an artefact may influence embodied interaction, interpretation, and experience.
